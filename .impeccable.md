## Design Context
### Users
Home beginners in tropical Indonesia, learning to grow food in small spaces using soil or hydroponics. English and Indonesian are equally supported.
### Brand Personality
Natural, encouraging, thoughtful. Help people feel capable, curious, and calm.
### Aesthetic Direction
Warm cream, forest green, terracotta, botanical drawings, editorial serif headings paired with a readable sans serif. Light theme, generous space, restrained motion, practical visual learning.
### Design Principles
- Make the next gardening action clear.
- Put approachable lessons before technical detail.
- Use step-linked 3D to explain what is hidden below the surface.
- Keep all learning functionality available on mobile and without WebGL.
- Respect local climate differences and avoid guaranteed outcomes.
